What is Hour of Lust?

Hour of Lust is a curated series of intimate drag performances produced by House of Lust. Each show pairs two or three performers for a tight, theatrical hour — a distilled experience built for emotion, spectacle and connection. We celebrate queerness, humor and the art of transformation.
